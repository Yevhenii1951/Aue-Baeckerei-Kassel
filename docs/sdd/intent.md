# Intent

Purpose: PORTFOLIO. Tier: Standard.

Aue-Baeckerei-Kassel is a portfolio-grade full-stack project for a modern craft
bakery in Kassel. It should feel like a real German small-business product:
warm, local, mobile-first, legally aware, and operationally useful.

The public experience starts with the bakery promise: "Morgens frisch, abends
bestellt." Customers can discover the assortment, understand allergens and
prices, preorder for the next morning, choose pickup or delivery options, and
see cafe, catering, and subscription offers.

The technical showcase is the ordering logic behind the atmosphere: cutoff at
20:00 Europe/Berlin, pickup-slot capacity, cart totals, product filters,
allergen metadata, and an admin backliste that aggregates tomorrow's bake plan.

Phase 1 should prove the product story without pretending to be a production
payment or delivery platform. External integrations such as Stripe, PayPal,
Klarna, SMS, courier tracking, Google Reviews, Instagram, and AI helpers remain
mocked or deferred until the core demo is clean.
