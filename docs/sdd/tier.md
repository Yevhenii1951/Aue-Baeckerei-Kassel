# Project Tier

Purpose: PORTFOLIO

Tier: Standard

## Why

Aue-Baeckerei-Kassel is a portfolio project for a modern bakery in Kassel. It
should demonstrate a polished customer experience, realistic German small
business constraints, and selected full-stack business logic without attempting
to ship every possible production integration in the first phase.

## Standard Scope

- German-first public site with local SEO, Impressum, Datenschutz, and mobile
  ordering calls to action.
- Product catalogue with realistic bakery categories, filters, allergen data,
  and demo-mode data.
- Vorbestellung flow with cutoff and slot-capacity logic.
- Cart and checkout simulation first; real payment integrations stay behind
  feature groups.
- Staff/admin area for orders, baking list, lightweight catalogue/content work,
  and analytics mock data.
- Tests for behavior that computes: cutoff date selection, slot capacity,
  totals, allergen/filter logic, and admin permission boundaries.

## Out Of Phase 1

- Real Stripe, PayPal, Klarna, SMS, courier tracking, and AI image upload.
- Production legal copy.
- Real Google Reviews or Instagram embeds without consent handling.
